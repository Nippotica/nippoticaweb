# Nippotica -- a Tokyo-based deeptech venture builder

We identify technologies ready to cross from research into production, build companies around them, and take them to market.

> **We turn advanced research into practical solutions for industry.**

Our network spans leading research institutions in Japan, Europe, Americas, and Singapore. Our clients are major Japanese manufacturers and multinationals integrating transformative technology into their operations.

# How Nippotica Builds

We follow a structured model: identify a promising technology, validate it through real market deployment, and develop it into a standalone business — typically within three to five years. Each business unit owns its domain, its products, and its clients.

Today that means two units: [**Nippofin**](https://www.nippofin.com/), building synthetic data and market signal products for quantitative finance and insurance; and [**Algotechniq**](https://www.algotechniq.com/), building Physical AI systems for industrial and infrastructure applications on Nvidia platforms.

# The Team

[**Our founders**](https://www.nippofin.com/profiles/) have built and exited companies across derivatives trading, risk analytics, wireless communications, semiconductors, and industrial AI. They've run desks at JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, and BlackRock; led engineering teams at NEC, Ericsson, and Alcatel-Lucent; and founded ventures acquired by Texas Instruments and Microchip Technology. They know how to build — and how to sell.
