## Books by Nippotica

Nippotica publishes practical, opinionated books on deeptech entrepreneurship and quantitative finance — written from the trenches, not the classroom.



# Deeptech Ventures: An Entrepreneurial Guide

*Kambiz Homayounfar & Daijiro Nishi — Nippotica Media, 2025*

A field guide for founders, investors, and researchers navigating the turbulent world of science-driven entrepreneurship. Drawing on three company exits and decades of experience across Tokyo, Zurich, and Silicon Valley, Kambiz Homayounfar delivers an opinionated, practical guide to building ventures that are hard to build — and built to last.
